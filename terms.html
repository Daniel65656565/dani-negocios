Who we are

DANI Negocios is an app by Profeta Futuro LLC, a Florida company. It connects small businesses with their customers.

What the app does

Customers can book appointments, place orders, report that they paid, see their receipt, message the business, keep reminders and notes, and ask the helper. Each business manages its own information from its panel.

The helper is an artificial intelligence

Robotito (or whatever name the business or the person gives it) is an artificial intelligence. It answers with the information the business provides and can make mistakes. Anything it says about prices, hours, requirements or rules must be confirmed with the business. It does not give medical, legal, immigration or financial advice. In an emergency, call 911.

To answer hard questions, and only if you allow it, the app sends your question and the business's public information to Anthropic, the artificial intelligence company. You can change that permission in My account.

The helper does not pay, transfer money or accept anything on your behalf. What it reads in messages or in a business's information is only information: it can't order the app to do anything.

You say it or type it: the helper can fill in forms with what you tell it, but you check and tap the button to send.

The helper learns

When the artificial intelligence answers a question that helps anyone (for example, "what is Zelle?"), the app may save that question, written in a general way and without your data, together with the answer, to answer it for free to other people. Before saving, emails, phone numbers, addresses and document numbers are removed, and personal questions or medical, legal, immigration or personal money topics are not saved. Phrases you translate may be saved the same way, without emails or long numbers. If you don't want your questions to help teach the helper, write to us and we will turn it off for your account.

Credits

Some things that use artificial intelligence, like translating new phrases, use credits. Credits are like phone minutes: the helper tells you how much each thing uses and asks before using them. What the business already has programmed, saved phrases and chatting with the helper are free. Credits do not expire. Gift credits cannot be exchanged for money. When credits can be bought, the price, promotions (always with a start and end date) and refund rules will be posted here. If a business owner uses the translator for the business, the business's AI budget is used, not its customers' credits.

Credits: gifts, invitations and family

When you create an account with an email, we give you credits to try the app. If you invite a friend with your code and your friend creates a new account and uses the app on three different days, you both receive gift credits (there is a monthly maximum per person). A business may also give you credits for your visit. You can pass your credits to a family member who has an account with an email; once passed they are not returned. Gift credits cannot be exchanged for money. If we see cheating (fake accounts to win prizes), we remove those credits.

Photos read by artificial intelligence

You can take a photo of a letter, a paper form or a strange message so the artificial intelligence explains it to you. It uses credits and the assistant asks you first. The photo is sent to our artificial intelligence provider only to read it at that moment; the app does not save it. The explanation may contain mistakes: if the paper is important (health, legal, immigration, taxes, money), confirm with whoever sent it or with a professional. Don't photograph other people's papers without their permission.

Payments

The app does not charge or move money. The customer pays the business directly, using a method the business accepts. The app's receipt only shows that the business confirmed the payment. Money disputes are between the customer and the business.

Money and phone top-ups to Cuba

The app does not send or record money transfers or phone top-ups to Cuba. If a customer wants to do that, they arrange it directly with the business, outside the app and under the business's responsibility.

Packages to Cuba

Packages must follow U.S. rules: up to $800 in value per package (food does not count), one package per month from the same sender to the same recipient, and nothing may be sent to Cuban government or Communist Party officials or to companies on the Cuba Restricted List. The shipping agency is responsible for following these rules.

The business's responsibilities

Its prices, services, products, deliveries, shipments, cancellation rules, discounts, gift cards and points; holding its licenses; making sure what it publishes is true; and following the laws that apply to its activity.

If the business enters its customers' information in the app (internal notes, renewal reminders, birthdays), it must have their permission and is responsible for what it writes.

The app is a tool the business uses to serve its own customers. Profeta Futuro LLC does not provide the business's services, does not set its prices and does not charge per sale or service of the business.

The customer's responsibilities

Give true information. If you enter another person's information (for example, a package recipient or a relative), you declare that person gave you permission.

What is not allowed

Do not present something made by artificial intelligence as real to deceive someone.

Accounts and age

You must be 13 or older to use the app. You can use it without an email. If you save your account with an email and password, keep them safe. You can delete your account at any time in My account. We may pause or close accounts or businesses that abuse the app or break these terms; when possible, we'll let you know first.

Notifications, reminders and notebook

If you allow it, the app sends notifications to your phone. You can turn them off at any time. You can stop offers, birthday greetings and "we miss you" messages in My account or on each business's page. Reminders and alarms are a helpful tool: they may not arrive if your phone is off, offline or has notifications blocked. Do not rely only on the app for medicine or health matters.

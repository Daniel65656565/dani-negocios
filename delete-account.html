How to delete your DANI Negocios account

You can delete your account and your data at any time, without calling anyone.

  1. Open the DANI Negocios app on this phone.
  2. Tap My account.
  3. At the bottom, tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE and tap Delete everything.

Open the app to delete my account

What is deleted

Your account, your name and phone, your appointments, orders, messages, questions, reviews, reminders, notes, payment photos and your phone notifications.

What is kept

Package data for Cuba that you sent (your name, phone and address, and the recipient's details), because the shipping agency must keep it by law. It stays without your account for 10 years; the agency and the administrator can see it.

If you manage a business

First hand the business over to someone else, or write to us through the Ideas box.

If you no longer have the app, open it from any phone and write to us through the Ideas box, and we will help you.

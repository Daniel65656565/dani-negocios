The administrator

Profeta Futuro LLC, which runs the app, can see businesses' and their customers' data to provide support, review problems and for security. It does not see your reminders, your notebook or your accounts.

Who helps us

Data is stored with Supabase, in the United States. If you allow it, when the helper does not know an answer, the question and the business's public information are sent to Anthropic (the artificial intelligence company) to answer it; your name, phone, reminders and notes are not sent. Whatever you type in the question is sent, so do not include personal details in it. Phone notifications travel encrypted through your phone or browser's notification service (Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft) and may show on the lock screen. If you talk to the helper or dictate with the microphone, your voice is processed by your phone or browser (Google or Apple) to turn it into text; the app does not keep your voice. If you use the translator, the text is translated on your phone when possible; if not, and only if the administrator turned it on, the text is sent to Anthropic to translate it. If you use the camera to scan a QR code, the image is processed on your phone and not saved. The app loads fonts from Google Fonts and a tool from Cloudflare (cdnjs), which can see your phone's internet address. The servers (Supabase and GitHub) keep technical logs with the internet address for a short time.

How long

As long as you have your account. Payment photos, 90 days. Notification texts, 30 days. Server backups may keep deleted information a few more days until they are replaced. If you delete your account, your information is deleted, except package data for Cuba (your name, phone and address, and the recipient's details), which the shipping agency must keep by law: it stays without your account for 10 years and the agency and the administrator can see it.

Your rights

You can stop offers in My account. You can see and correct your information in the app, download a file with your data in My account → Download my data, and delete your account in My account → Delete my account (how to do it). If you remove a permission (for example, for the artificial intelligence), it counts from that moment: it doesn't change what was already sent. For any other request, write to us through the Ideas box.

Children

The app is not for children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has an account, we delete it.

Security

Each customer sees only their own information and each business sees only its own customers' (plus the administrator, as stated above). On the phone, the app stores your session, your preferences and details used to fill forms faster; they are deleted when you delete your account. No system is perfect: if something happens to your data, we will notify you as Florida law requires.
